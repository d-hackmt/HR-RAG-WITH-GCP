Regional Sales Summary — Q3

North America closed $6.1M in new bookings this quarter, up 18% year-over-year, led by strong enterprise renewals. EMEA bookings totaled $3.4M, slightly below target due to slower deal cycles in the DACH region. APAC remains the smallest region by revenue but posted the highest growth rate at 22%.

Sales leadership is piloting a new commission structure starting next quarter, tying a larger share of variable pay to net-new logo acquisition rather than renewals. Regional sales directors should submit updated territory plans by the 15th. This report is distributed to the sales organization and does not cover HR policies such as leave or reimbursement.
