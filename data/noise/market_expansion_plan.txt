Market Expansion Plan

The company is evaluating expansion into Southeast Asia and Latin America, with a formal go/no-go decision expected by end of Q2 next year. Initial market entry will likely be partner-led rather than a direct office opening, to reduce upfront capital risk.

A cross-functional expansion task force, including representatives from finance, sales, and legal, will produce a market entry brief for each candidate region. Regulatory and data residency requirements are a key open risk in both candidate regions. This plan is a business strategy document and does not describe employee travel expense policy, which is maintained separately by HR.
