PUBLIC HOLIDAYS POLICY

Policy Category: Holidays

1. Purpose
This policy lists the public holidays observed by the company each calendar year and how they interact with other leave types.

2. Number of Observed Holidays
The company observes 12 public holidays per calendar year, published on the HR portal by December 1st of the preceding year. The list is based on national and regional holidays for the employee's registered work location.

3. Floating Holidays
In addition to the 12 fixed public holidays, employees receive 2 floating holidays per year, which can be used on any working day of the employee's choosing (for example, to observe a cultural or religious holiday not included in the standard list). Floating holidays must be requested through the HR portal at least 2 working days in advance and do not carry forward to the next year.

4. Holidays Falling on a Weekend
If a public holiday falls on a weekend, it is generally not shifted to the nearest working day unless explicitly announced by HR for that year, in which case the adjusted date will be published alongside the annual holiday calendar.

5. Working on a Public Holiday
Employees required to work on a declared public holiday due to business needs (e.g., customer support coverage) are entitled to a compensatory day off within 30 days, in addition to any applicable holiday pay as required by local law.

6. Interaction With Annual Leave
As noted in the Leave Policy, a public holiday that falls within a period of approved annual leave is not deducted from the employee's annual leave balance; the leave balance is automatically adjusted.

7. Office Closure
The office (for employees not working from home) is closed on all declared public holidays. Employees working from home on such days are not expected to be reachable, the same as employees in the office.

8. Regional Variations
Employees based in different regions or countries may observe a different holiday list appropriate to their location; HR publishes a location-specific calendar for each office.
