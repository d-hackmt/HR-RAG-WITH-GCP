TRAVEL AND EXPENSE POLICY

Policy Category: Travel Expense

1. Purpose
This policy sets out the rules for business travel booking, per-diem allowances, and expense claims specific to trips, complementing the general Reimbursement Policy.

2. Travel Approval
All business travel must be approved by the employee's manager before booking, using the travel request form on the HR portal. Domestic trips require manager approval only; international trips also require department head approval.

3. Booking Process
Flights and hotels for approved trips should be booked through the company's designated travel partner where available, to consolidate billing and ensure duty-of-care tracking. Employees booking independently due to partner unavailability must retain all receipts for reimbursement.

4. Airfare Class
Employees below Director level travel in economy class for flights under 6 hours and premium economy for flights over 6 hours. Director level and above may book business class for international flights over 6 hours.

5. Accommodation Limits
Hotel accommodation is reimbursed up to a nightly cap that varies by city tier, published annually by finance. Employees are expected to book reasonable, business-appropriate accommodation within this cap; amounts above the cap require prior written approval.

6. Per Diem Allowance
Employees on approved business travel receive a daily per-diem allowance covering meals and incidental expenses, at a rate published on the HR portal, varying by destination country. Per diem is paid regardless of actual spend and does not require receipts.

7. Ground Transportation
Reasonable taxi, ride-share, or public transportation costs to and from airports, stations, and meeting locations are reimbursable with receipts, following the general submission process in the Reimbursement Policy.

8. Combining Business Travel With Personal Time
Employees may extend a business trip for personal travel at their own expense, provided the extension does not increase the cost of the original business airfare and is disclosed on the travel request form in advance.

9. Expense Submission Deadline
All trip-related expenses must be submitted within 15 days of returning from travel, shorter than the standard 30-day window in the general Reimbursement Policy, due to the higher volume and time-sensitivity of travel claims.
