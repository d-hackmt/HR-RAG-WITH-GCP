CODE OF CONDUCT POLICY

Policy Category: Code of Conduct

1. Purpose
This policy sets out the standards of behavior expected of every employee, contractor, and manager while representing the company.

2. Core Principles
All employees are expected to act with honesty, respect, and professionalism in every interaction, whether with colleagues, clients, partners, or the public, in person or online.

3. Respect and Non-Discrimination
The company does not tolerate discrimination or harassment based on race, gender, age, religion, disability, sexual orientation, or any other protected characteristic. Any employee experiencing or witnessing such behavior is encouraged to report it to HR immediately, through the confidential reporting channel described in Section 7.

4. Conflicts of Interest
Employees must disclose any situation where personal interests could conflict with the company's interests, such as financial stakes in a vendor or hiring a close relative into a reporting line. Disclosures should be made to HR and the employee's manager as soon as the potential conflict is identified.

5. Confidentiality
Employees must not share confidential company information, including client data, financial information, or unreleased product plans, with anyone outside the company without proper authorization, both during and after their employment.

6. Use of Company Resources
Company equipment, software licenses, and systems are provided for work purposes. Reasonable personal use is permitted (e.g., checking personal email) provided it does not interfere with work duties or violate any other section of this policy.

7. Reporting Violations
Employees can report suspected violations of this Code of Conduct confidentially through the HR portal's ethics reporting form, or by emailing the dedicated ethics mailbox. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is strictly prohibited and is itself a violation of this policy.

8. Consequences of Violation
Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination for cause, as referenced in the Notice Period Policy, depending on the severity and nature of the violation.

9. Social Media and Public Statements
Employees should avoid making public statements on behalf of the company without authorization, and should exercise good judgment when posting about work-related topics on personal social media accounts.
