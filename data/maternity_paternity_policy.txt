MATERNITY AND PATERNITY LEAVE POLICY

Policy Category: Maternity Paternity

1. Purpose
This policy outlines paid leave entitlements for employees becoming parents through childbirth, surrogacy, or adoption.

2. Maternity Leave
Employees who give birth are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave, which may begin up to 8 weeks before the expected delivery date. This entitlement applies to employees who have completed at least 80 days of service in the 12 months preceding the expected delivery date.

3. Paternity Leave
Employees whose partner gives birth are entitled to 2 weeks of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 3 months of the child's birth. Paternity leave may be split into two blocks with manager approval.

4. Adoption Leave
An employee who legally adopts a child under the age of 1 year is entitled to 12 weeks of paid adoption leave, starting from the date the child is placed with the employee.

5. Notification Requirements
Employees should notify HR and their manager of their expected leave at least 8 weeks in advance where possible, to allow for adequate handover and temporary coverage planning, coordinated similarly to the handover process in the Notice Period Policy.

6. Job Protection
Employees returning from maternity, paternity, or adoption leave are guaranteed reinstatement to the same role or an equivalent role with the same pay and seniority, in line with applicable local labor law.

7. Phased Return to Work
Employees returning from maternity or adoption leave may request a phased return, working reduced hours for up to 4 weeks at full pay, subject to manager and HR approval.

8. Interaction With Other Leave
Maternity, paternity, and adoption leave are separate from and do not reduce the employee's annual leave balance as described in the Leave Policy. Employees continue to accrue annual leave while on maternity, paternity, or adoption leave.

9. Probationary Employees
Employees on probation (see the Probation Policy) who become eligible for maternity, paternity, or adoption leave during their probation period have their probation period paused and resumed upon return, rather than counted against them.
