PROBATION POLICY

Policy Category: Probation

1. Purpose
This policy describes the probation period applicable to new employees and the process for confirmation, extension, or termination during that period.

2. Duration of Probation
All new full-time employees serve a probation period of 3 months (90 calendar days) from their date of joining, unless a different duration is explicitly stated in their offer letter.

3. Notice Period During Probation
During the probation period, either the employee or the company may terminate the employment relationship with 15 days' written notice, or payment in lieu of notice, whichever the terminating party elects. This is shorter than the standard post-confirmation notice period described in the Notice Period Policy.

4. Performance Review During Probation
Employees on probation receive a formal check-in with their manager at the 6-week mark and a final review before the end of the 3-month period. The final review assesses role competency, cultural fit, and alignment with team expectations.

5. Confirmation
If performance during probation is satisfactory, the employee's manager submits a confirmation recommendation to HR before the end of the probation period. Once confirmed, the employee moves to the standard notice period and full leave entitlements described elsewhere in this handbook.

6. Extension of Probation
In cases where performance is inconclusive rather than clearly unsatisfactory, the probation period may be extended by up to 2 additional months at the manager's discretion, with written justification shared with the employee and HR. An extension is not a guarantee of eventual confirmation.

7. Benefits During Probation
Employees on probation are eligible for sick leave and casual leave on a pro-rated basis from their date of joining, but are not eligible to apply for work-from-home arrangements (see the Work From Home Policy) until confirmed, except with special manager approval.

8. Training and Onboarding
All new employees complete a structured onboarding program during their first 2 weeks, covering company policies, tools, and role-specific training, coordinated by both HR and the employee's manager.
