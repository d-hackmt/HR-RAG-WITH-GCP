REIMBURSEMENT POLICY

Policy Category: Reimbursement

1. Purpose
This policy explains which work-related expenses employees may claim reimbursement for, the applicable limits, and the submission process.

2. Eligible Expense Categories
The following categories are eligible for reimbursement, subject to the limits below: internet and mobile bills for remote work, home office setup (one-time), professional certifications relevant to the employee's role, business travel (see also the Travel and Expense Policy for trip-specific rules), and client entertainment pre-approved by a manager.

3. Home Office Setup Allowance
Employees eligible for the Work From Home Policy may claim a one-time home office setup allowance of up to 15,000 (local currency) within their first 3 months of eligibility, covering items such as a desk, chair, or monitor. Receipts are required for all claims.

4. Internet and Mobile Reimbursement
Employees with a standing work-from-home arrangement may claim up to 1,500 (local currency) per month toward internet costs, and up to 1,000 (local currency) per month toward mobile bills, provided these are used substantially for work purposes.

5. Professional Development Reimbursement
Employees may claim up to 25,000 (local currency) per calendar year toward job-relevant certifications or courses, subject to prior manager approval before enrollment. Reimbursement is processed only after successful completion of the course or certification.

6. Submission Process
All reimbursement claims must be submitted through the finance portal within 30 days of the expense being incurred, with original receipts or digital invoices attached. Claims submitted after 30 days may be rejected at the discretion of the finance team.

7. Approval Workflow
Claims under 5,000 (local currency) require only direct manager approval. Claims of 5,000 or more require both manager and finance department approval before reimbursement is processed.

8. Non-Reimbursable Items
Personal expenses, fines or penalties, alcohol (except in pre-approved client entertainment contexts), and any expense without a valid receipt are not eligible for reimbursement.

9. Payment Timeline
Approved reimbursements are paid out with the next regular payroll cycle following approval, typically within 15 working days.
