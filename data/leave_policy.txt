LEAVE POLICY

Policy Category: Leave

1. Purpose
This policy defines the paid leave entitlements available to all full-time employees and the process for requesting and approving leave.

2. Annual Paid Leave
Every full-time employee is entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued at a rate of 1.5 days per completed month of service. Unused annual leave may be carried forward to the next calendar year up to a maximum of 10 days; any balance beyond that is forfeited on December 31st unless local law requires otherwise.

3. Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave does not carry forward to the following year. For sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days, a medical certificate must be submitted to HR within 5 working days of returning to work.

4. Casual Leave
Employees may take up to 6 days of casual leave per calendar year for personal matters that do not qualify as sick leave, such as family emergencies or personal errands. Casual leave requests should ideally be submitted at least 1 working day in advance, though same-day requests are accepted for genuine emergencies.

5. How to Apply for Leave
All leave requests must be submitted through the internal HR portal at least 3 working days in advance for planned leave (annual and casual leave). Sick leave may be reported on the day of absence and formalized in the portal upon return. Leave requests are approved by the employee's direct manager and are subject to team workload and business needs.

6. Leave During Notice Period
Employees serving their notice period (see the Notice Period Policy) may not take annual leave during the last 15 days of their notice period without explicit written approval from their manager and HR.

7. Unpaid Leave
In exceptional circumstances, employees who have exhausted their paid leave balance may request unpaid leave. Unpaid leave requests must be approved by both the direct manager and HR, and require at least 5 working days' notice except in emergencies.

8. Public Holidays
Leave taken on a day that is already a declared public holiday (see the Public Holidays Policy) is not deducted from the employee's annual leave balance.
