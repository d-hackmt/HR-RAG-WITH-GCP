NOTICE PERIOD POLICY

Policy Category: Notice Period

1. Purpose
This policy defines the notice period required when an employee resigns or when the company initiates termination of employment, outside of the probation period.

2. Standard Notice Period
Once an employee has been confirmed after probation (see the Probation Policy), the standard notice period is 60 days (2 calendar months) for both resignation by the employee and termination initiated by the company, except in cases of termination for cause.

3. Notice Period for Senior Roles
Employees at the Director level and above are required to serve a notice period of 90 days, given the additional handover and succession planning typically required at that level.

4. Payment in Lieu of Notice
Either party may elect to shorten the notice period through payment in lieu of notice. If the company initiates this, the employee receives their full salary and benefits for the unserved portion of the notice period. If the employee requests early release and the company agrees, the employee may be required to forfeit salary for the unserved period, at the company's discretion.

5. Handover Requirements
During the notice period, the departing employee is expected to prepare a written handover document covering ongoing projects, key contacts, and access credentials, to be reviewed with their manager no later than 10 days before the last working day.

6. Leave During Notice Period
As stated in the Leave Policy, annual leave may not be taken during the final 15 days of the notice period without written approval from both the manager and HR, to ensure adequate handover time.

7. Termination for Cause
In cases of termination for cause (e.g., serious misconduct, breach of the Code of Conduct Policy), the standard notice period does not apply, and termination may be immediate, subject to applicable local labor law and internal disciplinary procedures.

8. Exit Formalities
All notice period completions are followed by the formal exit process described in the Exit Process Policy, including final settlement, asset return, and exit interview.
