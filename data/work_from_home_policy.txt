WORK FROM HOME POLICY

Policy Category: Work From Home

1. Purpose
This policy outlines the eligibility, expectations, and process for employees working remotely on a regular or occasional basis.

2. Eligibility
Employees who have completed their probation period (see the Probation Policy) and whose role does not require a fixed physical presence (e.g., front-desk, warehouse, or facilities roles) are eligible to work from home, subject to manager approval.

3. Standard Work From Home Allowance
Eligible employees may work from home up to 2 days per week as a standing arrangement, agreed with their manager. Employees are not permitted to work from home every day as a default arrangement; teams are expected to maintain in-office collaboration on at least 3 days per week unless the role is designated fully remote in the employment contract.

4. Occasional Work From Home
In addition to the standing 2-day allowance, employees may request occasional additional work-from-home days (e.g., for a home repair appointment or a focus-heavy task) by informing their manager at least 1 day in advance via chat or email. These occasional requests are not tracked formally but should be reasonable in frequency.

5. Fully Remote Roles
Certain roles may be designated fully remote at the time of hiring or by later agreement with HR and the hiring manager. Fully remote employees are expected to be available during core working hours (10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time) and to attend in-person team gatherings when requested, with reasonable notice.

6. Equipment and Expenses
The company provides a laptop and any role-specific software required to work from home. A one-time home office setup allowance may be claimed through the Reimbursement Policy, subject to the limits defined there.

7. Expectations While Working From Home
Employees working from home are expected to be reachable during standard working hours, attend scheduled meetings with video on where appropriate, and maintain the same productivity standards as in-office work. Repeated unavailability during working hours while marked as "working from home" may result in the arrangement being revoked.

8. Manager Discretion
Team managers retain discretion to temporarily suspend work-from-home arrangements for specific projects that require in-person collaboration, with at least 1 week's notice to affected employees where possible.
